    <h1 class="post-title"> Welcome to TLC Ned </h1> 
    <p><img src="/assets/images/working_garden.jpg" alt="Man kneeling inside of an arched mesh garden, building steps" width="100%" style="float: middle"></p>
    <p>Based in Nederland, CO, <strong>Trees Land Critters</strong> <i>(aka TLC Ned)</i> is a company dedicated to helping you manage your land and animals responsibly. With more than a decade of professional experience in arboriculture, forestry, and land management, we serve properties across the Front Range foothills and mountain communities with an approach rooted in ecology: we work with the land, not against it.</p>
</div>
<div style="overflow: auto;">
    <p><strong>Trees.</strong> TLC Ned is owned and managed by Kieran Hunt, an ISA Certified Arborist and ISA Certified Utility Specialist. We provide safe, science-based pruning that supports a tree’s long-term health and structure. We also perform technical tree removals, brush disposal, and wildfire mitigation: thinning fuels and creating defensible space to help protect your home in fire-prone terrain.</p>
    <p><strong>Land.</strong> Beyond the trees, we help you steward the whole landscape: establishing native plants that restore habitat and thrive in our dry climate, designing water-wise xeriscapes that cut irrigation without sacrificing beauty, clearing invasive species that crowd out native growth, and keeping your grounds healthy and resilient over time.</p>
    <p><strong>Critters.</strong> And because mountain living means sharing space with wildlife, we build predator-proof enclosures and fencing to keep pets, poultry, and livestock safe from pests and predators large and small. Our enclosures bring peace of mind while minimizing impacts to local ecosystems.</p>
    <p>See our <a href="/services">services</a>, <a href="/about">about</a>, and <a href="/gallery">gallery</a> pages for more information about who we are and the services we offer.</p>
